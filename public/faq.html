What AI products does i3 offer? +
We offer several AI-driven solutions, including:
  • SmashSupport.AI: A customer support agent that reduces ticket volume by 90%.
  • Fitness AI Coach: A personalized virtual coach for fitness brands.
  • i3 LingoBridge: A universal language translator that enables real-time communication across languages.
How can I integrate AI solutions into my business? +
We follow a structured, six-step process to deploy AI agents, which includes identifying high-impact use cases, securing executive buy-in, and scaling solutions over time.
Can I customize an AI solution to match my business needs? +
Yes. Our solutions are modular and can be tailored to your workflow, tone of voice, and system integrations.
How is your AI different from generic chatbots? +
i3 AI agents are purpose-built with advanced learning models, deep integrations, and real-time adaptability—not simple scripts or templates.
Do your AI agents support multiple languages? +
Yes. Our AI models support real-time translation for global communication across text and voice.

AI agents are designed to handle repetitive and low-complexity tasks, freeing human employees to focus on higher-level work. They enhance productivity, but humans remain essential for strategic decision-making and complex problem-solving.
